How to Plan Wedding Decor

A practical starting point for couples planning a wedding in Pretoria.

Wedding decor decisions are easier when the venue, guest count and priorities are clear first. Use this guide to prepare the information that helps you compare full-service styling with hiring individual decor pieces.

A planning guide from MON Events & Bridal, Pretoria.

In this guide 1. Confirm the event details 2. Check the venue plan 3. Choose your styling scope 4. Set visual priorities 5. Prepare a useful brief Planning checklist
Step 1

Confirm the event details

Start with the date, venue, type of celebration and an approximate guest count. These basics shape the spaces you need to style and the number of tables or focal areas to consider. If some details are still changing, note what is confirmed and what is an estimate.

Step 2

Check what the venue already provides

Ask the venue which furniture, linen, lighting and setup support are included. Confirm access times, setup rules, restrictions on candles or attachments, and who will be the event-day contact. These details help avoid planning decor that cannot be used or does not fit the setup window.

Sketch the main areas—such as the ceremony space, entrance and guest tables—and note what is already in each one. This gives you a clearer view of where styling will make the biggest difference.

Step 3

Choose between full styling and decor hire

With full-service styling, you discuss the overall look and the details to be designed and set up. With decor hire, you choose individual pieces for your own event plan. Some couples prefer one approach; others want to explore both before deciding.

Step 4

Set a few visual priorities

Choose the moments guests will see and use most: an entrance or welcome sign, the ceremony backdrop, the couple’s table, or the guest tables. A short priority list helps keep decisions focused and gives your stylist or hire supplier a clear starting point.

Collect a small set of reference images and describe what you like about each one—perhaps the colour, flowers, candlelight, table layout or overall mood. Also share anything you want to avoid. A few specific references are more useful than a large, unfiltered folder.

Step 5

Prepare a clear brief before requesting a quote

Include your date, venue, event type, approximate guest count, style references and budget range. Mention which areas matter most and whether you are considering full styling, decor hire or both. If you have a floor plan or venue photos, have them ready to share when asked.

Before you enquire

Wedding decor planning checklist

  • Event date and type of celebration
  • Venue name, location and venue contact
  • Approximate guest count and number of tables, if known
  • Venue-provided furniture, linen and setup details
  • Top styling priorities and areas to decorate
  • Colour palette or a small set of inspiration images
  • Budget range and whether you want styling, item hire or both
  • Collection, delivery and setup questions for items you plan to hire
