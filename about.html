Our Story

Started with a single wedding, built on every one since.

MON Events & Bridal began with a simple idea — that a celebration should feel like the people it's for, not like a template pulled off a shelf. What started as styling for friends and family grew into a full studio, working across weddings, showers and corporate events, always with the same intention behind every piece placed on a table.

Today, our work spans full-service design and a curated collection of décor available to hire — but the philosophy hasn't changed since the first event we ever styled.
