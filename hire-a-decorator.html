Event decor questions

What types of events do you style?

MON Events & Bridal styles weddings, bridal showers, baby showers and corporate celebrations. Tell us about your event in the event styling enquiry form.

Do you offer decor hire as well as full styling?

Yes. If you would like to select decor pieces yourself, browse the decor hire collection and send a booking request for the items you need.

Where do you provide event decor?

MON Events & Bridal is based in Pretoria. Share your venue location and event date so we can confirm availability for your event.

What should I include when asking for a quote?

Include your event date, venue, event type, approximate guest count, preferred style and budget range. These details help us understand your celebration and respond with relevant next steps.

How can I prepare before choosing a decor style?

Start with your event date, venue, approximate guest count and the parts of the event you want to style. Our wedding decor planning guide walks through those decisions.
