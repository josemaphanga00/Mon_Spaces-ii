Privacy Notice

How we handle information you share with MON Events & Bridal.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This notice explains how MON Events & Bridal handles personal information submitted through this website. We use it to respond to enquiries and provide event styling and décor hire services. Please do not include passwords, identity numbers, payment card details, health information, or other sensitive information in form messages.

Who is responsible for your information?

MON Events & Bridal, based in Pretoria, Gauteng, South Africa, is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. For privacy questions or requests, email moninternational12@gmail.com.

What information do we collect?

The information depends on the form you use:

  • Contact enquiry: your name, phone number, and message if you choose to provide them. These fields are optional.
  • Event styling enquiry: your name, phone number, email address, event and setup dates, event type, guest count, venue details, theme, budget range, and requested services.
  • Décor hire request: your name, phone number, email address, selected items and quantities, event type and dates, collection or delivery choices, delivery address if applicable, setup choice, and damage waiver acknowledgement.

We receive this information directly from you. Contact form fields are optional. Other forms mark required fields; if you leave those blank, we may not be able to respond to or process your request.

When your browser loads this site, the website host and providers of external fonts and icons may also receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser, and requested page.

Why do we use it?

We use form information to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, discuss event requirements, check and manage décor hire requests, communicate about services you request, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use these enquiry forms to sign you up for unrelated marketing.

Who may receive it?

Our forms are submitted through Formspree, a form service provider. Formspree processes submissions for us, may store them in its service, and can send them to the email address configured for the form. Formspree states that it uses infrastructure in the United States and other countries where it operates. Read the Formspree Privacy Policy for its handling of information. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs; those providers may receive technical connection data when your browser requests those files.

How long do we keep it?

We keep enquiry and booking information only for as long as it is needed to handle your request, provide related services, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. Formspree may retain submission data under its own privacy policy and service settings. You can contact us to ask about information associated with your request.

Your choices and requests

You may ask us to confirm whether we hold your personal information, provide access to it, correct it, or delete it where applicable. You may also object to processing in circumstances provided by law. Email moninternational12@gmail.com with your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

If you believe your personal information has been handled in breach of POPIA, you may contact the South African Information Regulator at POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za or visit the Information Regulator contact page.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. Please use the forms only for information needed for your enquiry; do not send sensitive personal information through them.

Updates to this notice

We may update this notice when our website or information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.
